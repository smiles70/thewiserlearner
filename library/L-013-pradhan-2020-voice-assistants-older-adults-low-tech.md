# L-013 — Pradhan, Lazar & Findlater (2020). Voice Assistants & Older Adults with Low Tech Use

## Why this is in the library

This is the **most directly relevant primary study** for Mynaani's premise:
older adults who are **not** already heavy technology users can adopt and benefit
from voice-driven AI. The authors deployed Amazon Echo devices into the homes of
seven adults aged 65–93 with low prior technology use for a 3-week longitudinal
study (interviews + interaction logs).

It is the single best citation for the claim that the channel's target viewer —
an older adult who is genuinely new to AI — is a real, studied population that
can succeed with conversational AI **when the design and onboarding fit them**.

## Core claims used by the contract

1. **Older adults with low prior tech use can and do adopt voice AI.** Adoption is
   not predicted by prior technology fluency.
2. **The conversational modality lowers the barrier.** Speech replaces the GUI
   navigation that older adults find effort-expensive (see L-007, L-012).
3. **Repair strategies are the bottleneck.** When the assistant misunderstands,
   older adults' ability to "rephrase, retry, recover" determines success more
   than vocabulary or accent.
4. **Trust is built through small, repeated successes**, not from feature lists
   or marketing claims.
5. **Use cases that succeed are concrete and meaningful**: weather, music,
   information lookup, hands-busy operation, reminders — not novelty or
   entertainment for its own sake.

## How the contract should use this source

- Cite as **direct evidence** that the channel's premise (teach AI to older
  adults who are new to it) is empirically supported.
- Cite when defending the contract's rule that **every video models
  recovery from error** — what to say when Claude misunderstands, gets it
  wrong, or says it doesn't know. Pradhan et al. is the warrant.
- Cite when defending the rule that **first lessons use concrete, immediately
  useful queries** (weather, recipes, simple lookups) before any abstract
  capability is introduced.
- Cite when defending the rule that **the voice/conversational modality is
  privileged** over GUI demonstrations for first-time AI users.
